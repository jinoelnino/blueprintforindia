IDEA 02 Children & Community · Bangalore

Bangalore's Parks.
Open to Everyone.

"The venues already exist. We just never opened them."

This is what it looks like today

Beautiful. Maintained. Barely used.

Bangalore has over a thousand public parks. Most of them are genuinely well kept — open sky, old trees, benches, green space that belongs to all of us. And most of them sit empty, day after day, because nobody thinks of a public park as somewhere they're actually allowed to use — not just walk through.

A child's birthday. A grandmother's 80th. A small haldi ceremony. Not every family can afford a party hall — but every family deserves a place to celebrate. Right now there's no way to book a park for an afternoon, so that need goes unmet even though the space to meet it is sitting right there, unused.

"Not every family can afford a party hall. But every family deserves a place to celebrate."

What if you could book it?

A simple booking system. A few hours. A nominal fee. Under open sky.

Map every public park in Bangalore and list it on a simple app — photos, facilities, capacity. Let anyone book a few hours for a celebration: a child's birthday, a grandmother's 80th, a small haldi, a family get-together. A nominal fee covers the booking and funds the park's upkeep. Free or discounted for children's birthdays and lower-income families, always.

Nothing new needs to be built. The parks are already beautiful and already maintained — this just opens the door that was never really closed, just never offered.

It's already happening — just informally

Families already find a way. This just makes it official.

This isn't a hypothetical. Bangalore families are already turning park benches into birthday venues and haldi ceremonies — figuring it out themselves, one balloon string at a time, because the alternative is a party hall they can't always afford. A booking system doesn't invent this. It just makes it easy, reliable, and open to every family.

What if we just did this

How it works in practice.

  1. 01

    Every public park mapped and listed on one app

    Photos, facilities, capacity, and allowed activities for every park in the city — so families know exactly what they're booking before they show up.

  2. 02

    Book a slot for a few hours

    A birthday, an anniversary, a haldi, a small family function. Pick a park, pick a time slot, book it — the same way you'd book anything else.

  3. 03

    A nominal fee, not a party-hall price

    Far below what a commercial venue charges — enough to fund upkeep, not enough to price out the families who need this most.

  4. 04

    Free or discounted for children's birthdays and lower-income families

    A child's birthday shouldn't be a budget decision. Public parks become a dignified, affordable venue for the celebration every family deserves.

  5. 05

    Booking fees fund the park's own upkeep

    The system pays for itself — the more a park is used and loved, the better maintained it becomes.

  6. 06

    Basic setup and clean-up handled on site

    Park staff manage turnaround between bookings, so the space is ready for the next family and returns to public use afterward.

  7. 07

    A park that's booked every weekend is a park that stays loved

    Active use is what keeps a public space cared for and valued by the people around it, long after the ribbon-cutting is forgotten.

It already works elsewhere

Places that have done this.

Worldwide
National park booking systems

National park systems around the world already run on slot booking for capacity and upkeep — proof that a booking layer makes a public space more usable, not less accessible.

Bangalore
Puttenahalli Lake community management

Active community management turned Puttenahalli Lake into one of Bangalore's best-loved public spaces — proof that when people actually use a space, it stays cared for.

Singapore & UK
Shared public grounds by design

Both treat public open space as something meant to be booked and used for community and family occasions, not just walked past — the same shift this idea makes for Bangalore.

What needs to happen

Who does what to make this real.

  • BBMP
    List parks on a booking system and set the fee structure A straightforward policy decision — no new construction, no new land. Parks already exist and are already maintained; this just opens a booking layer on top.
  • Park Staff
    Handle setup and turnaround between bookings Light coordination to keep the park ready for the next booking and returned to normal public use afterward.
  • App Operator
    Build and run the booking platform A simple, reliable app listing every park with photos, facilities, and live availability — the single layer that makes the whole system usable.
  • Families
    Use it — for the celebrations that matter Every booking proves the demand and keeps the park loved. The best way to protect a public space is to actually use it.
